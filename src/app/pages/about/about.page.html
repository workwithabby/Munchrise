What is Munchrise?

Munchrise is a breakfast food ordering app. Pick from our menu of sandwiches, rice meals, breads and smoothie bowls, add them to your cart, check out and follow your order — all from your phone.

It is a school project for IT 005 – Integrative Programming and Technologies, built by students who love a good morning meal.
